01 / WHY WE EXIST

Not a rerun.
A new beginning.

JTV is an independent creative concept network for stories that take risks. We believe great entertainment can be outrageous, funny, frightening, beautiful, and unexpectedly transformative.

Our characters don't have the luxury of controlling the world. They still get to decide what to do next. Sometimes they choose courage. Sometimes they choose chaos. Sometimes they make an absolutely magnificent mess.

We are developing a growing lineup of original concepts, show worlds, characters, and visual experiences. The future belongs to stories nobody has dared to tell yet.
